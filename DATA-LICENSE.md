# Data license

The data in `data/` combines the sources below.

## GuildWiki.de — CC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Skill acquisition (capture bosses, trainers, quests), mission data (rewards, bosses, foes, allies),
quests and the story flow, daily and weekly rotation lists, collector and material data, and the German-to-English name mapping are derived from
[GuildWiki.de](https://www.guildwiki.de), © GuildWiki.de contributors, licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes: pages were extracted through the wiki's API, parsed into structured records, names were
translated to English using each page's English-wiki link, German prose was dropped, and the
records were combined with gw-skilldata.

As required by the ShareAlike term, this derived data is released under the same license,
**CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. You may share and adapt it for non-commercial purposes, with attribution
to GuildWiki.de and this site, under the same license.

## Guild Wars Wiki (English) — GNU FDL 1.2

The storyline order and mission numbers for Prophecies, Factions and Nightfall come from the
[Guild Wars Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars.com)'s "List of <campaign> missions and primary quests" pages,
© Guild Wars Wiki contributors, licensed under the
[GNU Free Documentation License 1.2](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/fdl-1.2.html). Archived copies
from the Wayback Machine were used.

## GWiki (French) — CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 FR

[CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 FR](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.0/fr/), decide which quests appear
in each campaign's story flow.

## gw-skilldata — MIT

Skill stats, English skill names and descriptions, and German skill names come from
[build-wars/gw-skilldata](https://github.com/build-wars/gw-skilldata) under the MIT license.

## Game content

Skill names, descriptions, other game text and the icons in `img/` are ArenaNet's content;
see `NOTICE.md`. They are not covered by CC BY-NC-SA.
