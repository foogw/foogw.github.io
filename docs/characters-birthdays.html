My Characters

When your characters were made, how old they are, and when a check of /age narrows it

AboutAccountsCharactersOverviewBirthdays

/age tells a character's age in whole months, or whole days while it is under two months old. Each reading narrows the time it can have been made in; it helps while that age is about to tick over somewhere inside that time. Hold a reading's button to set it; Undo takes it back for five minutes.
