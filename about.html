About

A quick Guild Wars reference, made by a player for players

The things you look up most while playing Guild Wars: skills, storylines, missions, heroes, builds and today's rotations, in a few fast pages built from the community wikis and tools.

How it works

The site is plain static pages with no accounts, cookies or tracking. The builder keeps your saved builds and shortlist in your own browser only.

Mistakes and ideas

Data can be wrong or out of date. If you spot something, or have an idea, see Contact.
