About

A quick Guild Wars reference, made by a player for players

The things you look up most while playing Guild Wars, in a few fast pages built from the community wikis and tools: today's dailies and rotations, guides, storylines, areas, missions, quests, dungeons and maps, skills, bosses, heroes, armor, builds and a skill bar builder, and titles.

Your characters

On My Characters you can keep track of your accounts and characters: elites captured, missions, dungeons and areas done, titles, heroes and builds. The site then marks what each character still needs across its pages, says what's worth doing next, and on Today which of your characters today's dailies are for.

How it works

The site is plain static pages with no accounts, cookies or tracking. Everything you enter, your characters' progress and the builder's saved builds included, stays in your own browser only: use Backup to keep a copy, or to move it to another machine or browser (where it can be set read-only).

Mistakes and ideas

Data can be wrong or out of date. If you spot something, or have an idea, see Contact.
