Changes: pages were extracted through the wiki's API and parsed into structured records; names were translated to English using each page's link to the English wiki; German prose was dropped; the records were combined with gw-skilldata.

Guild Wars Wiki (English) — GNU FDL 1.2

The story flows for Prophecies, Factions and Nightfall follow the "List of <campaign> missions and primary quests" pages of the Guild Wars Wiki, © Guild Wars Wiki contributors, licensed under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.2. The wiki was offline, so archived copies from the Wayback Machine were used, and each story step links to its archived page.

GWiki (French) — CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 FR

The story flow uses GWiki, © GWiki contributors, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 2.0 FR, to check each quest's type and campaign: a quest the French wiki lists as secondary, or under another campaign, is left out of that campaign's flow.
