The efficient GWAMM

Focus

Missions and vanquishes: they carry the most titles at once. Every mission with its bonus, in order, progressing through the game rather than being run. Normal mode first, all of it; hard mode by the Zaishen days, for the reward and the people doing it that day.

On the way

Capture every elite in the mission and around its outpost. Map as you go, and vanquish while you map in hard mode. Carry the storybook, normal or hard mode, and fill it (a page can be bought within 30 days if you forgot). Recruit the heroes a line brings, and take the reputation along in the Eye of the North.

Prerequisites

The meta hero team before any hard mode: three Mesmers, two Necromancers, a Ritualist (Razah fills in). The port towns unlocked, to reach the other campaigns. The Eye of the North early, for its heroes. The 200 attribute points, and the Checklist's other marks as they come.
