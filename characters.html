Accounts and characters

Pick the character you're playing: its ticks are the ones the whole site shows and changes, on Skills, Missions, Dungeons, Areas and their pages. Click a character's name to pick it, an account's name to rename it; Done editing hides the buttons to rename, reorder and delete (Edit brings them back). Account titles are kept on the account, shared by its characters. All of it is kept in this browser only: see Backup to keep a copy or move it to another browser.
