Accounts

Each account holds its characters, its account titles (shared by them, ticked on a character's Titles tab) and its Reforged Coins, counted once it has the bell. Name it here; a new character goes on an account from the + by its name on Characters. An account is deleted once it has no characters. All of it is kept in this browser only: see Backup.
