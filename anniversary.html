Anniversary planner

The easy Zaishen Missions around an Anniversary Celebration: which to take ahead, which to do any time during it, which to wait for

Missions

      During the Anniversary Celebration every Zaishen Mission quest pays double, and a character gets one Proof of Legend per region for any mission done there, so one mission per region is worth doing, the easiest. A Zaishen quest taken from the board keeps for the whole event: the easy missions that come up in the last days before it can be taken ahead and handed in once it starts; the ones that come up during it are waited for; the rest of the list can be done on any day of it, with that day's quest.
