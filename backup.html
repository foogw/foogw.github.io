Your progress is kept in this browser only!
This is a static site, so your progress lives in your browser, not on a server. If you lose your device or clear this browser's data, you lose it. Download it or copy it now and then to keep it safe. Use it also to move between computers or browsers, but remember that each one keeps its own copy: a change made in one isn't seen in the others until you load it there.

Your saved progress is broken: the site can't read it. The pages go without it until it's fixed, and nothing here writes over it. Copy or download the text below to keep it, and please tell me about it, with the text. Then you can paste a fixed version and use it, or reset everything and start afresh.

What's in it

  • Your accounts: their names and order, account titles, and the Reforged Coins' clock
  • Your characters: their details, ticks, titles, heroes and builds, and when they were made
  • The Builder's saved builds
  • The guides: the steps you ticked, and the faction cap calculator's choices
  • Rotations: times shown in UTC or yours

Not in it: the character picked, accounts folded, the tab open and other choices of how a page looks.
