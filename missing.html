What's Missing

The gaps we know about. If you can help fill one, see Contact.

  • Map windows for some areas and missions Most are instanced areas whose map the game files don't place on a continent.
  • Two areas in one map window Where two areas share one terrain, both pages show the whole of it, e.g. Tasca's Demise and Spearhead Peak.
  • What's where on the maps Map windows don't show yet where NPCs, bosses, chests or quest items are.
  • Dungeons The Eye of the North dungeons and the pre-Searing dungeons have no pages yet.
